# Preparing your website for AI Search with the basics done right

AI search has created a lot of noisy advice. The useful part is much simpler: make the website technically clean, helpful, and easy for search systems and humans to understand.

Google's Search Central guidance says AI Overviews and AI Mode rely on the same basic SEO practices used for classic Search. There is no special schema, text file, or secret markup required to appear in these AI features.

## The practical checklist

Start with the basics that still compound:

- Make important pages crawlable and indexable.
- Keep core service content in visible text, not only images.
- Use descriptive titles, headings, internal links, and page summaries.
- Add structured data only when it matches visible content.
- Support important pages with original images or video where useful.
- Connect Search Console and analytics before launch.

## Why this matters for service businesses

AI search often answers more specific, comparison-heavy questions. A weak service page that only says "best website company" will not help much.

A stronger page explains:

1. What the service includes.
2. Who it is for.
3. What the process looks like.
4. What proof or examples support it.
5. What the next step should be.

## What Finlec would improve first

For most business websites, the highest-impact updates are:

- Service pages with clear scope and outcomes.
- Local trust signals such as address, contact details, and project context.
- Fast mobile layout with no overlapping UI.
- FAQ sections that answer real buying questions.
- Conversion tracking for forms, calls, WhatsApp taps, and lead quality.
