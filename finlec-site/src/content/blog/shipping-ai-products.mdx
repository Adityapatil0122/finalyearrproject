# Shipping AI products that don't embarrass you

Building an AI demo is easy. Shipping an AI **product** that doesn't hallucinate, leak data, or quietly burn $40 in tokens per session — that's the work.

## What we always wire in

- **Evals on day one.** A growing folder of golden conversations and adversarial prompts.
- **Cost & latency budgets.** Per-route. Surfaced in dashboards, not just logs.
- **Guardrails.** Input filtering, output validation, and a hard kill switch.
- **Observability.** Every prompt, response, and tool call traced.

## Why teams fail

They demo on cherry-picked happy paths. They never write the eval. They forget that LLMs are non-deterministic systems running on someone else's GPU.

The fix is unglamorous: treat your AI feature like a production database — capacity-planned, monitored, and tested.
