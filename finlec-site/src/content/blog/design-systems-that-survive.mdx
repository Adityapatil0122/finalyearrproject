# Design systems that survive a hundred PRs

Most design systems rot. Eighteen months in, the Figma library and the codebase have drifted. Buttons have nine variants. Colours are hardcoded in three places.

## Three rules

1. **Tokens are the API.** Never reference a hex value outside the token file.
2. **One source of truth.** Codify the system in code; let Figma read from it.
3. **Make the right thing the easy thing.** Lint for hardcoded values.

A system that can't be enforced won't be.
