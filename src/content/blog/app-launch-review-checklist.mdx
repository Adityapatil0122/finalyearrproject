# App launch checklist before App Store and Google Play review

Submitting an app is not just uploading a build. Store review checks whether the app is complete, stable, trustworthy, and ready for users.

Apple's developer guidance emphasizes privacy, security, safety, and reliability. Google Play release guidance encourages internal, closed, open, and production testing tracks so teams can catch issues before full rollout.

## Before review

Prepare these items:

- Final app name, screenshots, description, and support contact.
- Privacy policy and data handling details.
- Demo account details if login is required.
- No placeholder screens, broken links, or temporary content.
- Tested payments, subscriptions, or gated features if present.
- Crash-free build tested on real devices.

## Release carefully

Use testing tracks before production:

1. Internal testing for quick QA.
2. Closed testing for trusted testers.
3. Open testing when wider feedback is useful.
4. Staged production rollout after confidence improves.

## What Finlec checks

Our app launch checklist usually includes:

- First-run onboarding.
- Permission prompts.
- Offline and poor-network behavior.
- Push notification flows.
- Analytics events.
- Store listing assets.
- Support and feedback routes.
