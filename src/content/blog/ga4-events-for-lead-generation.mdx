# GA4 events every lead-generation site should track

Traffic is only useful when it turns into qualified business. A website rebuild should therefore include a measurement plan before design approval, not after launch.

Google Analytics recommends events for lead generation that go beyond a single form-submit conversion. That matters because many businesses close leads offline through calls, WhatsApp, demos, or sales follow-ups.

## Track the obvious actions first

Start with these events:

- `generate_lead` when a visitor submits a form or request.
- Phone tap clicks on mobile.
- WhatsApp button taps.
- Email link clicks.
- Service page CTA clicks.
- Quote, demo, or consultation requests.

## Add lead quality stages

If the sales team can update lead status, track these stages too:

1. `qualify_lead` when the lead fits your ideal customer profile.
2. `working_lead` when the team starts active follow-up.
3. `close_convert_lead` when the lead becomes a customer.
4. `close_unconvert_lead` when the lead does not close.

This turns analytics from a display dashboard into a tool for better decisions.

## What to decide before launch

Before publishing, define:

- What counts as a lead.
- Which channels need separate tracking.
- Which form fields are needed for qualification.
- How sales outcomes will be passed back into reporting.
- Which events should be marked as key events.
