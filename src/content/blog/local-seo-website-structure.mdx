# Local SEO starts with website structure

Local SEO is not only Google Business Profile posts and backlinks. The website still needs to make the business easy to understand.

For service companies, the structure should answer five questions quickly:

1. What do you do?
2. Where do you work?
3. Who do you help?
4. Why should someone trust you?
5. What should they do next?

## Build pages around intent

Good local service pages are specific:

- Website development in Pune.
- App development for startups.
- WhatsApp Business API setup.
- UI/UX design for ecommerce.
- AI chatbot for customer support.

Each page should include the service, use case, process, proof, FAQs, and a direct contact path.

## Add trust signals

Local buyers look for confidence before they ask for a quote:

- Office address and map.
- Phone and WhatsApp number.
- Client logos.
- Project examples.
- Clear service scope.
- Real team or business information.

## Internal links matter

A strong site does not leave pages isolated. Link between:

- Service pages.
- Case studies.
- Blog guides.
- Contact page.
- Client page.

This helps visitors move naturally and helps search engines understand the site.

## Keep technical basics clean

Before spending on campaigns, check:

- Page titles and descriptions.
- Fast mobile load.
- Crawlable navigation.
- Image alt text.
- Structured headings.
- Sitemap and Search Console.
